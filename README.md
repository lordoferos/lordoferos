## Hi, I'm Levi 👋

I lead survey and evaluation work in agriculture and food systems, and I've been doing it for about eight years across Kenya, Uganda, Tanzania and Rwanda. That covers household surveys, impact evaluations, soil health assessments and field data collection, from the questionnaire all the way to the final analysis.

Most of what I'm good at is the unglamorous part that decides whether a study holds up: instrument design, enumerator training, getting the same households back for the next round, and catching data problems while the team is still in the field instead of three months later. I currently run [Wrangla360](https://wrangla360.com), a research and data quality consultancy based in Nairobi.

Before Wrangla360, I was a data analyst on [asset-based community-driven development](https://www.cifor-icraf.org/abcd/), [agroecological transformation](https://www.cgiar.org/initiative/agroecology/) and [holistic metrics for agroecology](https://www.cgiar.org/news-events/news/the-measure-of-agroecology/). My background is in statistics. I write most of my analysis in R, use Python when it's the better tool, and like maps more than is probably healthy.

- **[Evidence Engine](https://www.wrangla360.com)** — checks Kobo/ODK survey data while fieldwork is still running and produces signed evidence packages for donors. Built at Wrangla360; currently hosted there rather than open source.
